How we work

01 → 07: The same process, every project.

Seven ordered stages run every engagement, so you always know what's happening next and why.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We map the business goal, users and technical constraints before a screen gets designed.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    We turn the goal into a scoped plan — features, timeline, and a fixed quote.

  3. 03

    Design

    Wireframes become a clickable prototype fast, so decisions get made on something real.

  4. 04

    Development

    The same team that designed it writes the code — clean, tested and documented.

  5. 05

    Testing

  6. 06

    Launch

    We deploy on your infrastructure of choice with zero-downtime rollout.

  7. 07

    Support

    We stay close for the first weeks in production, fixing rough edges as real usage comes in.

What it's like to work with us.

Questions

Before you reach out.

The things most founders and product leads ask us in the first call.

How long does a website take?

A marketing site usually ships in 4–6 weeks. Larger product builds run 8–14 weeks. You'll get a week-by-week timeline before we start.

Do you build custom websites?

Yes — every project is designed and coded from scratch around your brand and goals. We don't resell templated themes.

Can you integrate AI?

Yes. We build AI chatbots, document processing, predictive analytics and workflow automation, and can integrate them into an existing site or product.

Do you provide backend development?

Yes — REST APIs, authentication, databases and cloud deployment are all part of our Cloud & Backend service.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Absolutely. We regularly rebuild aging sites — like our Norvanta case study — without losing existing SEO equity.

Do you provide maintenance?

We include 2–4 weeks of post-launch support in every plan, and offer lightweight monthly retainers after that.

What technologies do you use?

Primarily HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js and Express, with Python for AI/ML work and PostgreSQL or SQLite for data.

How do I start a project?

Fill out the contact form below or chat with Virexo AI — either way, you'll hear back within one business day with a clear next step.
